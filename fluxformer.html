● Recorded model calculations

Each simulated condition is a computer-generated nutrient environment, defined by 20 uptake limits. A metabolic simulation calculates target reaction fluxes under those limits. FluxFormer receives the same limits and predicts the fluxes. “Low” and “high simulated growth” refer to the simulation’s resulting growth rate.

About these calculations and the data

The trained E. coli core model uses 95 reaction tokens, 128 embedding features, eight attention heads, and three Transformer layers. Every calculation includes all 95 reactions. The visible reactions are a view of that complete computation.

The four conditions were selected for illustration from simulated test data. Targets are simulation outputs, not experimental measurements. FluxFormer approximates the simulator; it does not enforce mass balance or reaction bounds at inference.

Traces were checked against the unchanged PyTorch model. All 340 per-sample checks passed at absolute and relative tolerances of 2 × 10−5. The historical test-generation seed and exact pFBA settings were not saved with the CSV.
