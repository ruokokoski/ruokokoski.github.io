I am a Doctoral Researcher in Computer Science at the University of Helsinki, focusing on machine learning, Transformer architectures, and AI agents. My research involves developing neural networks for scientific modeling, using simulated data to reduce the need for costly real-world experiments.

My current work centers on FluxFormer, a Transformer-based neural surrogate for genome-scale metabolic modeling. The long-term goal is to develop models that can predict metabolism across different microorganisms and environmental conditions, with potential applications in biotechnology and sustainable bioprocessing.

Before moving into data science, I worked as an RF and electronics design engineer. This background continues to influence my approach to research, combining an interest in the theoretical foundations of machine learning with practical applications and the development of intelligent AI systems.
